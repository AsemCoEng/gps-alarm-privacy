GPS Alarm Privacy Policy

Developer: ASEM HASSAN
App: GPS Alarm
Effective date: August 24, 2026
Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GPS Alarm handles information when you use the app. GPS Alarm is designed to create location-based alarms that can continue working while the app is in the background.

1. Information the app uses

Precise location. GPS Alarm uses your device location to:

  • show your position on the map;
  • calculate the distance to saved alarm destinations;
  • detect entry into an alarm area;
  • trigger a location alarm at the radius you selected; and
  • re-arm automatic alarms after you leave the configured area.

The app may access location while the app is not visible or the screen is off when you have active location alarms. Background location is used only to provide the location-alarm functionality you enable.

Saved alarm information. The app stores information such as alarm name, destination coordinates, alarm radius, whether the alarm is enabled, whether automatic re-arming is enabled, and alarm state. This information is stored locally on your device.

2. Search and map services

When you use place search, the text you enter and location information used to bias search results may be sent to the configured geocoding/search provider. The current production configuration may use Geoapify for place and address search. Geoapify processes those requests under its own privacy terms.

GPS Alarm also uses third-party mapping and location technology, which may include MapLibre, OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap-derived map data, EOX map imagery/services, and Google Play services for Android location/geofencing. Requests to third-party services may expose standard network information such as your IP address and requested map or search resources to those providers.

3. Permissions

Depending on your Android version and enabled features, GPS Alarm may request precise/approximate location, background location, notifications, foreground service access for tracking and alarm playback, vibration, wake-lock capability, and full-screen alarm notification capability where supported.

You can manage these permissions through Android Settings. Disabling a required permission can prevent some GPS Alarm features from working.

4. Data sharing

ASEM HASSAN does not sell your personal information. GPS Alarm does not currently send your saved alarm list to a developer-operated server.

Information is transmitted to third-party providers only as necessary to provide functions such as map display, geocoding/place search, and Android location services, subject to the providers' own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data retention and deletion

Saved alarms remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can delete individual saved alarms from within GPS Alarm.

Because the current version does not create a GPS Alarm user account or store your alarm data on a developer-controlled server, there is currently no separate online account deletion process.

6. Children

GPS Alarm is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly operate a service intended to collect personal information from children through user accounts.

7. Security

GPS Alarm is designed to minimize developer-controlled data collection by keeping saved alarms on the device. No method of electronic processing or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, especially when third-party network services are used.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when GPS Alarm features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.
